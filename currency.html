How the rate works

The calculator retrieves the latest available HKD/PHP reference rate from Frankfurter and converts automatically as you type. Reference rates are generally updated on business days, not continuously tick-by-tick.

Your bank, card provider, ATM or money changer may use a different rate and may add fees. Treat this as a planning estimate, not a guaranteed transaction quote.
