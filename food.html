HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Eat the classics, not just the famous names

0 of 16 must-try items checked

Choose one or two signature items at each stop. Hong Kong and Macau are snack cities: small portions and shared plates make it easier to try more without turning every meal into a food marathon. Prices are planning ranges, not menu quotes; confirm current prices and opening hours before visiting.

Best fits with your itinerary
• Mong Kok base: Kam Wah pineapple bun, Ki Tsui traditional pastries, Kai Kai dessert (Jordan).
• Central day: Tai Cheong egg tart, Tsim Chai Kee wonton noodles, Yat Lok roast goose.
• Macau day: Senado-area noodles and snacks; if you go to Taipa/Cotai, add a pork chop bun or Lord Stow tart.
HONG KONG · SAVOURY MUST-TRIES

What to order

Roast goose (燒鵝)

Signature Cantonese roast

Crisp lacquered skin, rich meat and plum sauce. Order with rice; char siu or roast pork are good additions.

Yat Lok · Central · 1 MICHELIN Star 2026

Great fit on Dec 14 after the Central sights. Expect a busy, fast-turnover meal.

Dim sum (點心)

Breakfast / lunch

Start with har gow (shrimp dumplings), siu mai, steamed rice rolls and baked BBQ pork buns. Share a few baskets.

Tim Ho Wan · Sham Shui Po · Bib Gourmand 2026

Worth the short MTR detour if you want an iconic, value-focused dim sum meal.

MICHELIN listing ↗

Wonton noodles (雲吞麵)

Quick local lunch

Look for springy thin egg noodles, shrimp-filled wontons and a clear, savoury broth. A classic quick meal.

Tsim Chai Kee · Wellington Street, Central · Bib Gourmand 2026

Fits neatly after Man Mo Temple / PMQ on Dec 14.

MICHELIN listing ↗

Claypot rice (煲仔飯)

Dinner · best in cooler weather

Rice cooks in the pot with sausage, chicken or other toppings; scrape up the toasted, crunchy rice at the bottom.

Kwan Kee Claypot Rice · Queen's Road West · Bib Gourmand 2026

Better as a dedicated dinner than a rushed snack stop.

Street snacks

Mong Kok wandering

Try curry fish balls, egg waffles, siu mai on skewers and cart noodles. Buy a small portion and keep moving.

Mong Kok / Ladies' Market / Temple Street

Perfect for Dec 17 when you are already exploring the markets. Choose busy stalls with fresh turnover.

Congee & rice rolls

Comfort food

Silky rice porridge with fish, pork or preserved egg; cheung fun rice rolls are soft and savoury with soy sauce.

Trusty Congee King · Wan Chai · Bib Gourmand 2026

Useful breakfast or gentle meal if you want a break from heavier roast meats.

HONG KONG · PASTRIES & SWEETS

Make room for these

Egg tart (蛋撻)

Shortcrust vs puff pastry

Tai Cheong is known for its buttery, cookie-like shortcrust; compare it with a flaky puff pastry tart if you spot one fresh from the oven.

Tai Cheong Bakery · 35 Lyndhurst Terrace, Central

Easy to pair with Man Mo Temple / PMQ on Dec 14.

Pineapple bun with butter (菠蘿油)

Cha chaan teng classic

Despite the name, there is no pineapple. The crackly sweet crust resembles pineapple skin. Try it warm with a thick cold slab of butter and Hong Kong milk tea.

Kam Wah Cafe & Cake Shop · 45–47 Bute Street, Mong Kok

Very close to your hotel area; an easy breakfast or afternoon snack.

Wife cake & old-school pastries (老婆餅)

Bakery souvenir

Flaky pastry around a soft winter-melon filling; also look for walnut cookies, banana cake and red-bean glutinous rice cakes.

Ki Tsui Cake Shop · 135 Fa Yuen Street, Mong Kok

A particularly convenient stop on your Dec 17 Mong Kok market day.

Milk pudding & tong sui (糖水)

Dessert after dinner

Try double-skin milk pudding, tofu pudding, black sesame soup or mango pomelo sago. Pick one bowl to share.

Kai Kai Dessert · Jordan · Bib Gourmand 2026

Convenient after Temple Street / Jordan on Dec 17.

MACAU · LOCAL FLAVOURS

Portuguese, Macanese & Cantonese

Portuguese egg tart (葡撻)

Macau pastry icon

Flaky layered pastry, rich custard and a blistered caramelised top. It is different from the smoother, paler Hong Kong egg tart.

Lord Stow's Bakery · original shop, 1 Rua do Tassara, Coloane

The original bakery is a detour from the historic peninsula; if staying in the old town/Cotai corridor, use the Taipa or Venetian outlet instead.

Story & outlets ↗

Pork chop bun (豬扒包)

Macau street-food classic

A thick, marinated pork chop in a crusty roll. It is simple, juicy and best eaten hot; some shops use softer bread or a pineapple bun.

Sei Kee Café · Taipa / Macau locations

Order the pork chop bun with the café's clay-pot coffee or milk tea. A great add-on if you visit Taipa Village.

African chicken (非洲雞)

Macanese signature

Chicken with a richly spiced, coconut- and chilli-tinged sauce reflecting Macau's Portuguese and global trading influences. Often served with rice.

Traditional Macanese / Portuguese restaurants

Consider a shared main at Restaurante Litoral Taipa if you extend your Macau day into Taipa.

Minchi (免治)

Home-style Macanese

Seasoned minced beef and/or pork with diced fried potatoes, often topped with a fried egg. Comforting, savoury and filling.

Macanese restaurants · old town or Taipa

Ask for a half portion or share it with another dish if you are also tasting snacks.

Bacalhau cakes (馬介休球)

Portuguese influence

Golden fried cod-and-potato fritters. A good starter to share before a Portuguese or Macanese main course.

Portuguese / Macanese restaurants

Look for them on a traditional menu rather than assuming every café serves them.

Serradura (木糠布甸)

Saw-dust pudding

Chilled layers of whipped cream and finely crushed biscuits. Light, sweet and an easy dessert to share.

Portuguese and Macanese cafés

Also try almond cookies or ginger milk pudding for a different Macau sweet.

YOUR TRIP · FOOD ROUTE

Best food stops by day

Dec 13 · Mong Kok → Tsim Sha Tsui

Breakfast/snack: pineapple bun at Kam Wah if timing allows. Harbour: coffee and an egg tart/pastry near K11 MUSEA. Keep dinner flexible around Tsim Sha Tsui.

Dec 14 · Central & The Peak

Lunch: wonton noodles at Tsim Chai Kee or roast goose at Yat Lok. Snack: Tai Cheong egg tart. Both are easy to combine with your Central walking route.

Dec 15 · Disneyland

Eat inside the park to save travel time. Choose one signature snack and one proper meal rather than leaving the park for food.

Dec 16 · Macau

Historic centre: noodles/congee near Senado, pork jerky and local snacks while walking, then a Portuguese egg tart. Add Taipa Village for pork chop bun / Portuguese meal only if time and ferry schedule allow.

Dec 17 · Diamond Hill & Mong Kok

Lunch near Nan Lian / Chi Lin, then market snacks in Mong Kok. Buy wife cake or traditional pastries at Ki Tsui; finish with Kai Kai dessert near Jordan / Temple Street.

Dec 18 · Departure

Keep breakfast simple near the hotel or airport. Avoid a long queue before the A21.

Practical ordering tips

• At dim sum, share 2–3 baskets per person to start, then add more.
• Some traditional shops are cash-first and may have brisk shared-table seating.
• Tea charges / service charges may apply at sit-down restaurants; check the bill.
• Ask about peanuts, shellfish, pork, dairy and cooking stocks if you have allergies or dietary restrictions.
• For egg tarts and buns, buy when fresh and eat warm; pastry texture drops as it cools.
• In Macau, the original Lord Stow bakery is in Coloane, not the historic centre. The Taipa and Cotai outlets are more convenient if you are short on time.
