POSE BREAKDOWN

How to recreate it

1

Shift your weight

Put most of your weight on one leg. Let the other leg relax slightly forward or outward so the stance feels asymmetrical, not stiff.

2

Keep one hand in a pocket

Rest one hand casually in a trouser pocket. Keep the other arm loose by your side; avoid pressing both arms tightly against your body.

3

Angle your hips and shoulders

Turn your body just a little away from the camera, then bring your face back toward the lens. A small angle adds shape without looking forced.

4

Relax your face

Try a genuine smile or laugh rather than holding a fixed expression. Keep your chin level and shoulders dropped.

5

Leave space around you

Ask the photographer to step back enough to include your shoes and some surroundings. Keep the camera around waist-to-chest height and avoid cutting off your feet.

Before you take the shot

HONG KONG & MACAU PHOTO IDEA

Where to try this pose

Look for a simple textured wall, a quiet edge beside greenery or a sunlit lane. In Hong Kong, try the quieter edges around Tai Kwun or the open promenades near Tsim Sha Tsui. In Macau, the pastel façades around the Historic Centre make a colourful alternative. Check that you are not blocking a walkway.
