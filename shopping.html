WHERE TO SHOP

Best fit by area

👟 Sneakers Street · Fa Yuen Street, Mong Kok

Best match for trainers and sportswear. Compare exact model codes, sizing, condition and shop receipts before buying.

👕 Langham Place + Ladies’ Market · Mong Kok

Useful for browsing mainstream fashion, accessories and lower-cost clothing in one neighbourhood. Treat market quality and bargaining as variable.

📷 Sim City · Mong Kok

Specialist camera, audio-visual and computer accessory shops. For expensive tech, verify model number, local/regional warranty, included accessories and return policy.

🛍️ Harbour City + K11 MUSEA · Tsim Sha Tsui

Good all-round mall stop for clothing, shoes, accessories and brand comparisons, close to the waterfront.

🎁 Shoppes at Venetian · Cotai, Macau

Large indoor mall with fashion, accessories and international brands. Best as an optional Cotai add-on, not a reason to rush the historic centre.

🧢 Sham Shui Po · optional specialist detour

Worth adding only if electronics components, cables, small gadgets or fabrics are a priority; it is a separate district rather than a quick Mong Kok stop.

FIT IT INTO YOUR ITINERARY

Suggested shopping windows

Day 1 · Sun, Dec 13 — Tsim Sha Tsui (light browse)

After hotel check-in and before/after Avenue of Stars, browse K11 MUSEA or Harbour City. Keep purchases light on arrival day.

Day 5 · Thu, Dec 17 — Mong Kok (main shopping block)

Best fit: your itinerary already includes Mong Kok markets. Add 60–90 minutes for Sneakers Street and Langham Place; choose one market rather than trying to cover every street.

Day 4 · Wed, Dec 16 — Macau Cotai (optional)

If energy and ferry timing allow after the historic centre, choose Shoppes at Venetian for an indoor browse. Skip it if it makes the Macau day rushed.

Day 2 · Mon, Dec 14 — Central / Sheung Wan (design-led)

PMQ is already on your route; allow a little extra browsing for local design, gifts and independent labels. Not the best day for a large shopping haul before Disneyland.

My recommendation: make **Day 5 (Dec 17)** your main shopping session, because Mong Kok is already in the plan. Use Day 1 for a quick Tsim Sha Tsui browse and keep Macau shopping optional.
BUY SMART

Quick checks before paying

  • For electronics, check the exact model, plug/voltage compatibility, warranty region, included accessories and return terms.
  • For sneakers and clothes, try them on and confirm the size label and condition.
  • Compare prices across more than one shop; “tax-free” does not guarantee the lowest price.
  • Keep receipts and photograph the model/serial number for higher-value purchases.
  • Leave room in your luggage and avoid buying bulky items on the first day.
