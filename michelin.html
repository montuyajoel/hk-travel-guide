TRIP FIT · RECOMMENDATION

My realistic MICHELIN shortlist

Dec 14 · Central: Yat Lok for a starred roast-goose lunch, or Tsim Chai Kee for a quick Bib Gourmand wonton-noodle meal. Choose one, not both if you want a relaxed day.

Dec 16 · Macau Peninsula: A Lorcha for a Bib Gourmand Portuguese/Macanese meal; The Eight if you want a luxury dim sum splurge. Add Lord Stow only if you are deliberately travelling to Coloane or can reach a convenient Cotai outlet.

Dec 17 · Jordan / Mong Kok: Kai Kai Dessert for a Bib Gourmand dessert stop after the markets / Temple Street.

For a 6-day trip, one starred meal plus two Bib Gourmand experiences is a good balance. The three-star rooms are extraordinary, but can consume a large part of the day and budget.
