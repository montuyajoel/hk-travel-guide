Your Da Nang itinerary

A day-by-day route with suggested stopovers, coffee shops and food breaks for 7–12 January 2027.

HOW TO USE THIS PLAN

One day at a time

Each day includes a suggested order of stops, practical timing, a nearby coffee break and a food idea. These are flexible suggestions, not reservations. For a group of five or more, compare one private vehicle with multiple ride-hailing cars.

7 Jan8 Jan9 Jan10 Jan11 Jan12 Jan

January can bring cooler, cloudy or rainy conditions in Central Vietnam. Keep outdoor viewpoints flexible and check access before setting out.

THURSDAY · 7 JANUARY

Arrival, My Khe Beach & Dragon Bridge

Keep the first day light. Check in, get your bearings and enjoy the coast before heading into the city for evening lights.

AFTER ARRIVAL

Airport → Cordial Beach Hotel

Check in at 30 Loseby Street, Son Tra. Arrange a group transfer if everyone arrives together.

SATURDAY · 9 JANUARY

Son Tra Peninsula & Marble Mountains

A nature and culture day. Start early for cooler conditions; check road access and weather on Son Tra.

MORNING

Linh Ung Pagoda & Lady Buddha

Explore the pagoda grounds and coastal viewpoints. Dress respectfully and follow any access restrictions.

LATE MORNING

Son Tra viewpoints

AFTERNOON

Explore caves, pagodas and viewpoints. There are steps and uneven surfaces; wear shoes with grip.

SUNDAY · 10 JANUARY

Hoi An Ancient Town & lantern evening

Allow most of the day. The old town is best explored on foot; stay through dusk for lantern-lit streets.

MORNING

Travel to Hoi An

Allow around 45–60 minutes depending on traffic. Agree a return pickup time with your driver.

LATE MORNING

Ancient Town lanes & Japanese Covered Bridge area

Explore heritage streets, old merchant houses, riverside lanes and photo spots. Check any heritage ticket requirements.

Old Town map ↗
LUNCH

AFTERNOON–EVENING

Save the riverside for golden hour and stay for the lantern atmosphere. Return to Da Nang after dinner.
