Group transport tip

For 5+ travellers, compare a private 7-seater or 16-seater with multiple Grab rides, especially for Hoi An, Son Tra and Ba Na Hills. Request a round-trip quote in advance.

Weather note
January can be cool, cloudy and rainy in central Vietnam. Keep a light rain layer and swap outdoor stops if conditions turn poor.

Eat like you're here

Food & café hit list

Mì Quảng

Turmeric noodles with herbs, crunchy rice crackers and a little broth.

Bánh xèo

Crispy savoury pancake, usually wrapped with greens and dipped in sauce.

Salted & coconut coffee

Try both styles at independent cafés around An Thuong and Son Tra.

Hoi An food to look for: Cao Lầu, White Rose dumplings and bánh mì. Market and restaurant prices vary; check menus before ordering for a large group.

Ways to save as a group

• Split a private vehicle between all travellers.

• Choose local eateries and market food for most meals.

• Prioritise free beaches, city walks and viewpoints.

• Treat Ba Na Hills as an optional paid upgrade.

• Confirm transport quotes and attraction ticket prices before paying.

Ba Na Hills can add a substantial ticket and transfer cost. Adding it may take the trip above €200 per person.
